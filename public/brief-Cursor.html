What is exaggerated

Rotor speed is 0.55 mechanical turns across the motor plate. A real high-pole machine would be a blur.

Spoke sag is drawn in pixels and labeled as screen sag. It is not a microstrain.

Tooth flanks are trapezoids. The pitch circles and the 6:1 speed ratio are the part that is exact.

Angle traces in the limb plate are the pose on screen. Violet torque is a schematic pulse, not a load cell.

Repurpose

The same five-plate stack (cover, motor section, reduction, sensor, limb) drops onto an elbow, a gripper, or a robot arm joint by swapping the link pose and the gear constants. The check script is the gate: ratio, clearance, and foot contact have to stay true.
